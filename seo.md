# Why You Keep Starting Over — Why We Become

## 🎬 About this video

You don't have a problem with starting. You have a problem with continuing.

Starting feels exciting. Continuing feels ordinary. This video is about the quiet middle — the part of change where nothing dramatic happens, nobody notices, and progress is invisible — and why that is exactly where most people quit and restart.

## 🔑 Key ideas

- **The Possibility Phase** — why a new beginning feels so good, and why that feeling is not evidence that this time will be different
- **The Repetition Wall** — most people don't struggle at the beginning; they struggle at the repetition
- **All-or-Nothing Thinking** — how one bad day gets mistaken for proof that the entire plan failed
- **Faster Recovery** — the goal isn't never to mess up, it's to return to the path before one bad day becomes one bad month
- **Worst-Day Routines** — build the routine you can keep on your worst day, not your best one
- **False Progress** — when changing the plan becomes a way of avoiding the work the plan requires
- **Evidence Stacking** — identity doesn't change through one giant decision, but through hundreds of small pieces of evidence
- **The Middle** — the quiet stretch where momentum is actually built
