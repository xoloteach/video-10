## Description

You don't have a problem with starting. You have a problem with continuing.

Every Monday, a new plan. New routine, new goals, new version of yourself. And for a few
days it works — until you miss one day, decide you've "already messed up," and quietly
drift back to where you began.

This episode is about the part nobody talks about: the middle. Why beginnings feel
exciting and repetition feels ordinary, why all-or-nothing thinking turns one bad day
into a full reset, and what it actually takes to keep going when the motivation is gone.
