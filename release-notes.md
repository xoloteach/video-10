# Why You Keep Starting Over

**Why We Become — Episode: Why You Keep Starting Over**

You don't have a problem with starting. You have a problem with continuing.

Starting feels exciting. Continuing feels ordinary. This episode is about the quiet middle — the part of change where nothing dramatic happens, nobody notices, and progress is invisible — and why that is exactly where most people quit and restart.
